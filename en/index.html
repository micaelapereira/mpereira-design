Delos Coffee House

A riverside café, told through a website.

  • HTML/SCSS
  • Bootstrap 5
  • Responsive
  • Specialty coffee

Delos Coffee House is a specialty coffee shop in Tigre, Buenos Aires. It was my final project for the Web Development course: a five-page, fully responsive site with a modular SCSS architecture (partials, variables and mixins) built on Bootstrap, SEO tailored to each page, and animations made with native transitions and AOS.

The challenge was to capture everything you feel when you visit Delos: its style, the menu, the design of the space, the riverside setting, and the care and dedication of its team. So that anyone browsing the site gets a taste of that experience and leaves with a lovely memory of it.

Agenda OM

Booking a moment of wellbeing, made easy.

  • Benchmarking
  • Research
  • Interviews
  • MVP
  • Wireframing
  • Prototyping
  • Testing
  • Wellness

Agenda OM is an app for booking wellness experiences: massages, reiki, family constellations and holistic group gatherings. It was my final project for the UX/UI Design course.

The challenge was to make a single app work for both one-on-one sessions and group gatherings, and to keep booking simple and frictionless.

Agenda OM mobile app process

Open any slide to see it full size

1

Research

  • Market research
  • Benchmarking
  • 2 user personas

I started by understanding the landscape: I researched the wellness experiences market, compared how other apps handle bookings, and created two user personas to always keep in mind who I was designing for.

2

Define

  • Problem, goal and solution
  • Storytelling
  • POV
  • MVP

With that foundation, I defined the problem, the goal and the solution, and told them as a story so I'd never lose sight of the people. That led to the point of view (POV) and the MVP: the minimum the app had to get right.

3

Structure

  • Card sorting
  • Information architecture
  • Taskflow
  • Userflow

I organized the app's content through a card sorting exercise, which shaped the information architecture, and mapped out the taskflow and userflow so booking would be a clear path from start to finish.

4

Design

  • Sketches
  • High-fidelity wireframes
  • UI Kit
  • Heuristic evaluation

I went from early sketches to high-fidelity digital wireframes, built a UI Kit to keep everything consistent, and reviewed the design with a heuristic evaluation.

5

Validate

  • Usability test with 5 users
  • Interview script
  • Satisfaction surveys
  • Efficiency and effectiveness metrics
  • Executive summary

I tested the final prototype with 5 users, using an interview script, satisfaction surveys and efficiency and effectiveness metrics. All five completed the three tasks, and confirming a booking took just a few seconds. Finding a class was the hardest part, which led to the recommendations in the executive summary, such as bringing all the filters together at the top of the home screen.
